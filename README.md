# STAT UN 1001 Fall 2026

This repository is the working space for developing and operating STAT UN 1001 for Fall 2026. It includes course design notes, syllabus drafts, learning outcome maps, weekly teaching materials, assignments, project materials, and administrative resources.

Current syllabus as of June 19, 2026: [`course-development/syllabus.md`](course-development/syllabus.md)

## Use of AI in Course Development

AI tools are used as course-development assistants to support planning, drafting, alignment, and review. The instructor remains responsible for all course decisions, student-facing materials, grading policies, and evaluation instruments.

AI support is used in three main ways:

- Learning outcome mapping: AI helps organize course learning outcomes, connect them to weekly lecture topics, identify gaps or redundancies, and align outcomes with activities, assignments, quizzes, projects, and exams.
- Course material preparation: AI helps draft and revise lecture plans, flipped activities, in-class activities, examples, datasets notes, handouts, Canvas-ready text, and other instructional materials.
- Evaluation support: AI helps draft and review quiz questions, exam prompts, project rubrics, grading guides, feedback language, and other evaluation instruments for clarity, alignment, and appropriate difficulty.

## Human Review

All AI-generated materials are reviewed and revised by a human before use. Human review checks disciplinary accuracy, course fit, accessibility, fairness, student privacy, alignment with learning objectives, and consistency with instructor expectations.

AI-generated content is treated as draft material, not as final authority. Final course materials reflect human judgment and revision.

## Repository Structure

- `course-development/`: syllabus drafts, course design documents, learning objective maps, instructional team guidance, and development notes.
- `course-materials/`: Canvas-ready weekly materials and teaching resources.
- `Assignments/` and `Projects/`: shared assessment and project materials.
- `Quizzes/` and `Exams/`: local-only assessment materials excluded from Git.
- `Admin/`: administrative course materials.
- `teaching-stories/`: examples, narratives, and teaching cases.
