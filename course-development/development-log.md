# STAT UN 1001 Development Log

This log records major course-design decisions for the workspace.

## 2026-06-10

- Created initial course-development workspace for STAT UN 1001 Fall 2026.
- Adopted OpenIntro Statistics as the primary textbook.
- Adopted selected chapters and ideas from Veridical Data Science, especially the data science life cycle and PCS framework.
- Framed the course as rigorous, accessible, and centered on statistical intuition, data-based claims, and responsible reasoning in an increasingly AI-shaped society.
- Set the instructional format as a semi-flipped classroom rather than a generic hybrid course.
- Established weekly student rhythm:
  - Flipped activity posted by Thursday.
  - Flipped activity due Monday evening.
  - Tuesday class focused on clarification, Q&A, and concept development.
  - Thursday class focused on activities, projects, peer review, or quizzes.
- Set grading breakdown:
  - Flipped activities: 10%.
  - Six biweekly quizzes, lowest dropped: 20%.
  - Mini-project tasks and peer review: 10%.
  - Final project: 20%.
  - Cumulative final exam: 40%.
- Decided quizzes will be paper-based, usually held on Thursdays, scanned, and graded in Gradescope.
- Decided quizzes should use formats similar to the final exam, while the final exam may include more variability in difficulty and more synthesis across topics.
- Set tentative quiz dates:
  - Thursday, October 1.
  - Thursday, October 15.
  - Thursday, October 29.
  - Thursday, November 12.
  - Tuesday, November 24.
  - Thursday, December 3.
- Moved Quiz 5 from Thursday, November 19 to Tuesday, November 24 as the Thanksgiving-week exception.
- Added policy that quizzes and exams may draw from assigned readings, learning materials in flipped activities, class discussion, and project activities.
- Chose Wednesday afternoon as the recommended weekly TA meeting time.
- Consolidated workspace into four canonical documents:
  - `syllabus.md`
  - `course-design-development.md`
  - `instructional-team-guide.md`
  - `development-log.md`
- Added a master week-by-week flipped activity plan to `course-design-development.md`.
- Decided to keep the semester-level flipped activity plan in the course design document, then split into separate weekly files only when drafting Canvas-ready activity materials.
- Replaced the compact flipped-activity table with week-by-week bullet sections covering learning objectives, flipped activity, Tuesday plan, Thursday plan, project arc activities, and suggested OpenIntro/VDS contents.
- Created `course-materials/week-01` through `course-materials/week-13` as placeholders for later Canvas-ready weekly materials.
- Added `learning-objective-map.md` as a separate companion document to map weekly objectives to flipped activities, Tuesday/Thursday activities, project arc work, and assessment evidence.
- Integrated `AI-activities-design.md` into the main planning materials by adding AI integration guardrails, a reusable AI claim critique template, and priority AI-aware activity seeds.
- Clarified that AI should be a recurring context for statistical reasoning, not the organizing structure of the whole course.
- Added instructional-team guidance for facilitating AI-aware activities without drifting away from statistical reasoning.
- Updated the syllabus to frame statistical reasoning as a critical skill for living, studying, and working in an increasingly AI-shaped society.
