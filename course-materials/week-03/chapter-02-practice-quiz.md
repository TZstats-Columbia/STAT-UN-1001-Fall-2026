# Week 03 Chapter 2 Practice Quiz

## Canvas settings

- Classic Graded Quiz configured as 0-point practice
- 20 automatically checked multiple-choice questions, each worth 0 points
- Every question includes a fifth **Just show me the answer** choice. Selecting it is marked incorrect and reveals the same immediate explanation, while allowing downloaded response data to distinguish answer requests from genuine incorrect attempts.
- One attempt
- Submitted answer, correctness, correct answer, and explanatory feedback shown immediately
- Wrong answers do not reduce the course grade; the preparation check-in awards the 10 completion points
- Students are encouraged to keep Chapter 2 open and use the question references to read while working
- Downloadable through Quiz Statistics and Student Analysis
- Unpublished until instructor review
