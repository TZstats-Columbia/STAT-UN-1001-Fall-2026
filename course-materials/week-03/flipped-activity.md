# Week 03 Flipped Activity: Chapter 2 Reading, Practice, and Questions

## Student workflow

This activity is graded for completion. Students should make a genuine attempt because the results will guide the instructor's in-class emphasis.

1. Read all sections of [OpenIntro Statistics, Chapter 2](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/).
2. Complete the **Week 03 Chapter 2 Practice Quiz** with the chapter open. The quiz deliberately sends students back to relevant figures, guided practices, and exercises.
3. Submit the quiz before reviewing its automatic feedback.
4. Complete the anonymous **Week 03 Preparation Check-In**.

The practice quiz is worth 0 points and reports the submitted answer, correctness, correct answer, and feedback immediately. Wrong answers do not reduce the course grade. Every practice question includes a **Just show me the answer** choice so students do not need to enter a random response merely to access the explanation. The anonymous preparation check-in awards all 10 completion points.

## Preparation check-in

The check-in asks students to report:

- how much of Chapter 2 they read and whether they reviewed the practice feedback;
- how difficult or scary the sample-mean equation feels;
- whether the barrier is notation, the meaning of *n*, abstraction/application, arithmetic, or something else;
- whether calculating the mean of 1, 2, 1, 2, and 4 helped;
- clarity of center/quartiles/mode, histograms/skew, 0/1 variables as proportions, and contingency tables/conditional proportions;
- what is clear and unclear about variance and standard deviation before the in-class lesson;
- which Figure 2.23-style display is easiest to understand and what makes the displays difficult;
- optional questions or topics for class.
