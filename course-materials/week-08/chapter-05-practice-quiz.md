# Week 08 Practice Quiz: Confidence Intervals and Decision Errors

This is a 0-point practice quiz with multiple attempts and immediate feedback. Try each problem before selecting **Just show me the answer.** The check-in, not the quiz, is the 10-point completion grade.

## Scope

- OpenIntro Statistics 5.1: point estimates and sampling variability (review)
- OpenIntro Statistics 5.2: confidence intervals for a proportion
- OpenIntro Statistics 5.3: the beginning of hypothesis-testing logic and Guided Practice 5.17

## Practice questions

1. **Name the model.** A random sample of 1,000 adults gives `p-hat = 0.887`. When the randomization and success-failure conditions are reasonable, what model describes the sampling distribution of `p-hat`? Explain what its center represents.
2. **Check the conditions.** Compute `n p-hat` and `n(1 - p-hat)` for the solar-policy sample. Why do these counts support a normal approximation?
3. **Figure 5.6 count.** Of 25 intervals generated when the true `p = 0.88`, how many capture the true value?
4. **Figure 5.6 lesson.** What is the correct lesson from the one interval that misses?
5. **Confidence level.** Why does a 99% interval use a larger critical value than a 95% interval, and what does that do to interval width?
6. **Example 5.8: margin of error.** With `p-hat = 0.887`, `SE = 0.010`, and `z* = 1.96`, calculate the margin of error.
7. **Example 5.8: endpoints.** Use your margin of error to calculate the 95% confidence interval.
8. **Example 5.8: interpretation.** Select the statement that correctly describes the interval in context.
9. **Guided Practice 5.9.** What does `z* = 2.58` tell us about a 99% interval compared with a 95% interval?
10. **Guided Practice 5.14.** Which statement correctly describes what “95% confident” means, and why might an old poll not describe current opinions?
11. **Guided Practice 5.17.** In the courtroom analogy, identify `H0`, `HA`, and the meaning of “fail to reject `H0`.”
12. **Decision errors.** Match each description to Type I or Type II error: rejecting a true null; failing to reject a false null.
13. **Bias versus variability.** Which issue is not fixed simply by increasing sample size: random sampling variability or a systematically biased sampling process? Explain.
