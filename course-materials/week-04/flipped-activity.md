# Week 04 Flipped Activity: Categorical Tables and Probability Preview

## Student workflow

1. Review OpenIntro Statistics Chapter 2.2 on categorical data displays and comparisons.
2. Read OpenIntro Statistics Chapter 3.1 and 3.2 on probability language, probability rules, Venn diagrams, and independence.
3. Complete the **Week 04 Practice Quiz: Categorical Data and Probability Preview** with the textbook open.
4. Review the automatic feedback. The feedback gives practice steps; those steps are for learning and will not be provided on a quiz.
5. Complete the anonymous **Week 04 Preparation Check-In**.

The practice quiz is worth 0 points and shows correctness and feedback immediately. Wrong answers do not reduce the course grade. Every multiple-choice practice question includes **Just show me the answer** so students can request the explanation without submitting a random content answer.

The anonymous preparation check-in awards all 10 completion points. Canvas records completion for grading while separating identities from the response text.

## Important boundary for students

Quiz 1 covers Lectures 1-5 and OpenIntro Chapters 1-2. The Chapter 3 probability questions in this flipped activity prepare students for Week 04 class discussion and later assessments; they are not part of Quiz 1.

## Practice quiz topics

- One-variable frequency tables and relative frequencies.
- Two-way tables, conditional proportions, and choosing the correct denominator.
- An advanced Chapter 2-style association question modeled on Exercise 2.22.
- Exercise 3.1-style true/false reasoning about chance processes and disjoint events.
- Guided Practice 3.7-style disjoint events and the addition rule, using cafeteria choices instead of dice.
- Guided Practice 3.14 and Exercise 3.7-style Venn diagram reasoning, using iPhone ownership and Apple computer ownership.
- Probability-rule checks for valid probability distributions.
- Guided Practice 3.20-style complement and "at least / no more than" reasoning, using notification counts instead of dice.
- Independence practice using hypothetical New York/Boston and Seattle/New York rain examples. The activity states that these numbers are invented for practice; real weather support will be investigated later.
- Exercise 3.11-style multiplication under an independence assumption, using a nongendered student-pair example.

## Preparation check-in topics

- Reading and practice completion.
- Clarity ratings for one-way tables, two-way tables, Venn diagrams, probability rules, complements, and independence.
- What feels hardest about probability notation or probability language.
- What students want to review before Quiz 1.
- What students want to ask about the Week 04 probability preview.
