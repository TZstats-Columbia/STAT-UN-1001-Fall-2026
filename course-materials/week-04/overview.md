# Week 04 Overview: Categorical comparisons, probability foundations, and Quiz 1

## Class dates

Sep 29 and Oct 1

## Learning objectives

- Create and interpret one-variable frequency tables and two-way tables.
- Use conditional proportions to compare categorical groups while naming limitations.
- Use basic probability language, Venn diagrams, complements, and independence checks.

## Prepare before class

Review OpenIntro Chapter 2.2, read Chapter 3.1-3.2, complete the Week 04 practice quiz, review the feedback, and submit the anonymous preparation check-in.

Main resources: OpenIntro Chapter 2.2 and Chapter 3.1-3.2; selected Veridical Data Science Chapters 4-5 ideas.

Quiz 1 covers Lectures 1-5 and OpenIntro Chapters 1-2. The Week 04 Chapter 3 probability preview is for class discussion and later assessments, not Quiz 1.

## Class plan

Tuesday: frequency tables for one categorical variable; two-way tables for two categorical variables; conditional proportions and an advanced categorical comparison; then transition to probability, Venn diagrams, probability rules, complements, and independence.

Thursday: project discussion and work time, followed by Quiz 1 during the final 15 minutes of class. Quiz 1 covers Lectures 1-5 and OpenIntro Chapters 1-2, not the Week 04 Chapter 3 probability preview. Students may bring one single-sided page of notes.

## Project and assessment connection

Complete Mini-project 1; Mini-project 2 will begin in Week 06.
