# Week 02 Flipped Activity: Worksheet, Reading, and Questions

## Student instructions

This activity is graded for completion. It is important that you try your best with your own efforts. The problems are designed to resemble problems that will appear on quizzes and exams. Your honest performance will help the instructor decide what to focus on in class, so please complete the activity by the deadline.

Complete the following steps in order:

1. Open and complete the **ChatGPT on Your Brain worksheet** in the Week 02 module. Do not check the summary while completing the worksheet.
2. After finishing the worksheet, check the summary and self-grade your work. Note anything you would now revise or explain differently.
3. Read all sections of [OpenIntro Statistics, Chapter 1](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/).
4. Complete the Week 02 Chapter 1 Practice Quiz. Submit your own first response before reviewing the automatic feedback.
5. Complete the Week 02 Preparation Check-In. Confirm your preparation, rate how comfortable you feel with the major Chapter 1 concepts, and enter any questions you would like the instructional team to address.

The preparation check-in awards all 10 completion points. The practice quiz is worth 0 points: it marks each answer correct or incorrect and provides immediate feedback, but wrong answers do not reduce your course grade.

## Preparation check-in questions

1. Which statement best describes your worksheet process?
   - I completed the worksheet before checking the summary and then self-graded it.
   - I completed part of the worksheet before checking the summary.
   - I checked the summary before completing the worksheet.
   - I have not yet completed the worksheet.
2. After self-grading, how much of the worksheet could you explain without help?
   - Nearly all of it
   - Most of it
   - About half of it
   - Less than half of it
   - I have not self-graded yet
3. How much of OpenIntro Statistics Chapter 1 did you read?
   - All sections
   - Most sections
   - Some sections
   - I have not finished the reading
4. Have you submitted the Week 02 Chapter 1 Practice Quiz and reviewed its automatic feedback?
   - Yes, I submitted it and reviewed the feedback.
   - I submitted it but have not yet reviewed the feedback.
   - I have not yet submitted it.
5. Rate each concept group using: **Ready to apply**, **Mostly comfortable**, **Somewhat confusing**, or **Very confusing or new**.
   - Cases, variables, values, data matrices, and variable types
   - Populations, samples, observations, statistics, and parameters
   - Sampling methods and sources of sampling bias
   - Observational studies versus experiments
   - Explanatory and response variables, association, causation, and confounding
   - Random assignment, control groups, placebo, blinding, and blocking
6. Optional: What questions from the worksheet, reading, or practice quiz would you like us to address in class?
