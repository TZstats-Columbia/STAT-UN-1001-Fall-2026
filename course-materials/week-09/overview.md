# Week 09 Overview: Hypothesis testing and statistical significance

## Class dates

Tuesday, November 3: no class. Thursday, November 5: class meets as scheduled.

## Learning objectives

- Construct and interpret a confidence interval for a population proportion, while checking conditions and separating precision from validity.
- Explain how confidence level, sample size, and sample variability affect interval width.
- State a null hypothesis and alternative hypothesis that match a research question.
- Interpret a test statistic as the observed statistic's distance from the null-model expectation, in standard-error units.
- Interpret a p-value as the probability of results at least as extreme as those observed, assuming the null model is true.
- Make a cautious conclusion: reject or fail to reject the null hypothesis, without claiming to prove either hypothesis.
- Distinguish statistical significance from practical importance, causation, and data quality.
- Identify Type I and Type II decision errors in context.
