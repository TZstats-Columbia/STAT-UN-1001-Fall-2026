# Week 07 Overview: From distribution models to foundations of inference

Dates: Tuesday, Oct 20 and Thursday, Oct 22

## Focus

Tuesday is a short bridge from the models in Chapter 4 to the foundations of inference in OpenIntro Section 5.1. We will work through one normal-model example and one binomial-model example, then use Section 5.1 to ask what a sample can tell us about a larger population and why a point estimate varies from sample to sample.

Thursday is a hands-on Mini-project 2 workshop using Gemini Notebook. Students will use their project question, source notes, and retrieved data to build an exploratory data story. The notebook is a thinking and organization aid; students remain responsible for checking definitions, calculations, graphs, and claims.

## Learning goals

By the end of the week, students should be able to:

- Set up a normal-model probability using a mean, standard deviation, and standardized value.
- Recognize and work with a binomial setting: fixed number of trials, two outcomes, stable success probability, and independence.
- Explain why a sample proportion or sample mean is a point estimate rather than the population parameter itself.
- Distinguish sampling variability from systematic bias.
- Explain how sample size affects the variability (standard error) of a point estimate.
- Identify the population, parameter, sample, statistic, and point estimate in a data story.
- Use Gemini Notebook to organize project sources and draft an exploratory analysis while independently verifying the statistical work.
