# Week 10 Overview: Comparing proportions and chi-square reasoning

## Class dates

Tuesday, November 10 and Thursday, November 12.

## Learning objectives

- Interpret a difference between two sample proportions while keeping the order of the groups clear.
- Explain what affects the uncertainty of a difference in proportions without calculating its standard error from a formula.
- Use a supplied confidence interval, test statistic, or p-value to reason about a difference between two population proportions.
- Explain expected counts as the counts predicted by a null model.
- Distinguish a chi-square goodness-of-fit question from a chi-square test of independence.
- Compare observed and expected counts to identify where data disagree most with a null model.
- Interpret a supplied chi-square p-value and write a cautious conclusion without claiming causation.

## Class plan

#### Tuesday

We will compare two proportions. The emphasis is on defining the two groups, keeping the subtraction order clear, checking the study design and conditions, interpreting confidence intervals, and using supplied standard errors or p-values. We will then introduce goodness-of-fit reasoning through observed and expected counts.

#### Thursday

We will continue with chi-square goodness-of-fit and independence questions. We will calculate selected expected counts and compare them with observed counts, but we will not carry out long chi-square calculations or use a distribution table. **Quiz 4 takes place during the final 15 minutes of class.**
