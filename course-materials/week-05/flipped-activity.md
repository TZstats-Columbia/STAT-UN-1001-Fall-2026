# Week 05 Flipped Activity: Probability Models, Random Variables, And What Repeats

Due: Monday, Oct 5, 8:00 p.m.

This activity prepares you for Week 05 class meetings on probability word problems, sampling with and without replacement, random variables, and continuous distributions. Some of this material is conceptually demanding. The goal is not to master every calculation before class. The goal is to arrive with enough shared language that we can practice and discuss productively.

## Learning Goals

After completing this activity, you should be able to:

- Distinguish joint, marginal, and conditional probability in a table.
- Use the phrases "with replacement" and "without replacement" to describe what changes after each draw.
- Explain how population size affects sampling without replacement.
- Translate a short word problem into a chance process, sample space, event of interest, probability sentence, and rule.
- Identify a random variable and interpret its expected value as a long-run average.
- Compare variability conceptually without relying on the variance formula.
- Interpret area under a continuous distribution as probability over an interval.

## Instructor Analysis Notes

Before Tuesday, scan responses for:

- Confusion between joint, marginal, and conditional probabilities.
- Whether students can name the denominator in conditional probability.
- Whether students treat sampling without replacement as independent in small populations.
- Whether students can explain why population size matters.
- Confusion between one random outcome, a probability distribution, and expected value.
- Whether students think the variance formula is required for use rather than a way to formalize spread.
- Whether students understand Section 3.5 at the level of "area over an interval."

Use Tuesday to prioritize discussion of denominators, chance processes, and replacement. Use Thursday to connect the same reasoning to random variables, expected value, and variance intuition.
