# Week 05 Overview: Probability practice, random variables, and distributions

## Class dates

Oct 6 and Oct 8

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish joint, marginal, and conditional probability in context.
- Use a probability tree to organize conditional probabilities and extend the same reasoning to more than two events.
- Translate probability word problems into a chance process, sample space, event of interest, probability sentence, and rule choice.
- Explain how sampling with replacement differs from sampling without replacement, and why population size matters.
- Use Google Sheets to produce simple summaries and tables.
- Explain a random variable in context.
- Use expected value to summarize a simple random variable.
- Compare variability conceptually without using the variance formula as a required procedure.

## Class plan

Tuesday: begin with a no-slides Google Sheets demonstration of simple summaries and tables. Then use the Lecture 7 practice deck to work through probability word problems, with emphasis on joint, marginal, and conditional probability, probability trees, and multi-stage chance processes.

Thursday: transition from probability models to random variables and distributions. Students define a random variable, interpret expected value, and connect chance variability to later project uncertainty language.

## Project and assessment connection

Probability practice and Mini-project 1 wrap-up only; no new project is due this week. Students should distinguish an observed pattern from a quantity that could vary under repeated sampling or a chance process.
