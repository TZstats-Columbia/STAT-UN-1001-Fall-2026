# Welcome to STAT UN1001

This course is a friendly introduction to statistical concepts and reasoning. We will learn how data-based claims are made, how they can go wrong, and how statistical thinking helps us reason responsibly about evidence.

## Start here

1. Read the [course syllabus](https://courseworks2.columbia.edu/courses/252546/assignments/syllabus).
2. Review the Week 01 module before the first class.
3. Check CourseWorks and your Columbia email regularly for announcements.
4. Bring questions to class or post general questions on the course discussion board.

## Normal weekly rhythm

- **Thursday:** the next week's flipped activity is posted.
- **Monday evening:** the flipped activity is due for completion credit.
- **Tuesday:** concept discussion, questions, examples, and clarification.
- **Thursday:** activities, applications, project work, or an in-class quiz.
