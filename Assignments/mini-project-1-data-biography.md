# Mini-project 1: Data Biography and Retrieval Audit

## Purpose

Investigate where retrieved data came from, what they measure, what they omit, and what claims they can support before beginning analysis.

## In-Class Work

1. Continue with the data from Mini-project 0 or retrieve a more suitable dataset using the [Data Search and Retrieval Assistant](https://pingpong.hks.harvard.edu/group/735?assistant=3600).
2. Identify the original data producer and source—not merely the assistant that helped retrieve it.
3. Document how observations were generated or collected.
4. Define the cases and each variable you expect to use, including units and whether variables are measured, counted, classified, or constructed.
5. Record geographic coverage, temporal coverage, aggregation level, missing values, filters, and exclusions.
6. Explain who or what is represented and what may be missing.
7. Write one claim the data could support and one claim they could not support.

## Submit

Submit a one-page data biography containing:

- Statistical question.
- Original data source and producer.
- Collection or generation process.
- Cases, variables, definitions, and units.
- Geographic, temporal, and aggregation coverage.
- Missingness, exclusions, and representation concerns.
- One supportable claim and one unsupported or overreaching claim.

Do not submit prompts, screenshots, or assistant conversation histories.

## Completion Criteria

Full completion credit requires clear evidence that you checked provenance, definitions, coverage, and limitations rather than treating the retrieved result as self-explanatory. Assistant use itself is not graded.
