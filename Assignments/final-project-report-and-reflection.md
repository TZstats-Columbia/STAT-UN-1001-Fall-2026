# Final Project: Report and Reflection

## Purpose

Put the course project sequence together in a short data investigation. Your final report should state a question, analyze relevant data, communicate findings clearly, reflect on the investigation process, and discuss limitations.

## Timeline

The final report and reflection are due Thursday, December 3. The final week of class is reserved for final exam review and cumulative practice.

## Submit

Submit a final report or equivalent statistical artifact containing:

- Statistical question and motivation.
- Data source, cases, variables, units, coverage, and important provenance details.
- One or more appropriate graphs, tables, or numerical summaries.
- Interpretation of the main finding in context.
- Uncertainty, variability, comparison, association, or prediction reasoning where appropriate.
- Limitations, including what the data cannot support.
- A brief reflection on how the question, data, analysis, or claim changed during the project.
- Data-source citation and any required AI-use acknowledgment for tools outside the two course-provided assistants.

Do not submit prompts, screenshots, assistant conversation histories, or records of assistant use.

## Grading

The final project is graded using a rubric. Strong submissions connect the question, data, analysis, conclusion, and limitations into one coherent statistical investigation.
