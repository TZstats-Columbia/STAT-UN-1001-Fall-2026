# Week 1 Course Entry Survey

## Student-facing survey

### Purpose

This short survey will help the instructional team understand what brings you to STAT UN1001, what you hope to learn, and what questions and experiences you bring to the course. There are no right or wrong answers. Thoughtful and candid responses will help us make examples, activities, and class discussions more relevant to the class.

### Privacy and grading

- The survey is worth **5 points for completion**.
- Your written responses are **anonymous**. Canvas records that you completed the survey so that you can receive credit, but your identity is not attached to your answers.
- Do not include your name or other personally identifying information in your responses.
- Answer every question in **at least 20 words**. Complete sentences are encouraged.
- Your views, prior experience, and opinions will not affect your grade. Credit is based on completing the survey and responding thoughtfully to every question.
- Estimated time: **12–15 minutes**.

### Questions

1. **Why are you taking STAT UN1001 this semester?** Describe any academic or program requirements as well as your personal reasons or motivations. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

2. **What do you most hope to understand or be able to do by the end of this course?** Include at least one concrete skill, question, or application that matters to you. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

3. **What prior experience have you had with statistics or data analysis?** You may include courses, workshops, research, work, independent learning, or everyday experiences. If you have had no prior statistics course, describe the closest related experience you have had. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

4. **Thinking about previous statistics, mathematics, or data-learning experiences, what felt frustrating, discouraging, unhelpful, or less effective for you?** If you have little prior experience, describe any concerns you have or approaches you hope this course will avoid. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

5. **What concerns you most about taking this course?** You might consider the workload, pace, use of AI, technical or computing skills, mathematical preparation, participation, assessments, or something else. Explain what support or course practices might help address your concerns. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

6. **For your intended education and professional journey, what statistics or data knowledge do you think you will need?** Describe where or how you imagine using that knowledge. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

7. **What are you curious about right now—inside or outside statistics?** Describe a question, topic, problem, or phenomenon and explain what makes it interesting to you. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

8. **What are your current thoughts about artificial intelligence?** Discuss at least one opportunity or benefit and at least one concern, limitation, or uncertainty. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

9. **What about artificial intelligence are you most curious to understand, investigate, or learn to do?** Explain why this question or capability matters to you. *(Minimum 20 words.)*

### Important limitations and checks

1. Canvas can record who completed an anonymous graded survey while separating identities from the written answers. The student-facing language should therefore distinguish **anonymous answers** from the non-anonymous completion record used to award credit.
2. Classic Quizzes does not reliably enforce a minimum word count for essay responses. The 20-word requirement should appear in the instructions and every question, but compliance cannot be checked student-by-student without weakening anonymity.
3. Download responses from the survey's **Quiz Statistics** area using the **Student Analysis** CSV. Remove or avoid any identifying metadata before analysis, if Canvas includes it in the export.
4. Each other flipped activity is currently worth 1 point. The published 5-point survey therefore counts five times as much as one flipped activity within the 10% Flipped Activities category. Review this relative weighting before final grades are calculated.

### Completion grading

- Canvas awards the five completion points when a student submits the graded survey; the essay responses themselves are not scored.
- The instructional team can audit submission status and completion scores without using the anonymous response text.
- The 20-word minimum is an instruction and good-faith completion expectation, not an automatically enforced or individually auditable grading rule.

### Suggested analysis plan

- Export the Student Analysis CSV and retain a read-only original copy.
- Create a working copy with one row per anonymous response and one column per question.
- Develop broad, non-evaluative themes after reading a sample of responses rather than assigning themes in advance.
- Summarize theme frequencies and representative anonymous excerpts; do not attempt to identify students from their writing.
- Use findings for course planning and instructional improvement only, consistent with the stated purpose of the survey.
